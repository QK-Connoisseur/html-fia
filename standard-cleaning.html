What is included every visit

Our maintenance clean keeps the essentials sparkling so you can enjoy more free time. The same lead cleaner returns whenever possible to learn your preferences.

  • Dusting from eye level down, including decor and flat surfaces
  • Full floor care: vacuum, sweep, and mop according to surface type
  • Bathroom refresh with disinfected fixtures, mirrors, and counters
  • Kitchen shine with wiped appliances, counters, and sink reset
  • Spot-check doors, light switches, and high-touch handles

Standard cleaning checklist

Here is the exact checklist our pros follow on every standard visit. Customize it by room or by priority.

Whole-home tasks

  • General dusting, including ceiling fans, blinds, and window sills within reach
  • Make beds and straighten linens (with fresh linens on request)
  • Empty bins, replace liners, and organize loose clutter
  • Polish mirrors, glass inserts, and interior windows within reach
  • Final walk-through to tidy throw pillows, blankets, and decor

Kitchen focus

  • Sanitize counters, backsplash, and high-touch cabinet fronts
  • Wipe exterior of appliances including microwave, oven, and refrigerator
  • Scrub sink, polish fixtures, and run garbage disposal with deodorizer
  • Spot clean pantry doors and breakfast bar seating
  • Launder a small load of kitchen towels on request

Bathrooms

  • Scrub and disinfect showers, tubs, and grout lines within reach
  • Shine glass doors, mirrors, and chrome fixtures
  • Wipe vanities, cabinet fronts, and organize counter items
  • Sanitize toilets inside, out, and around the base
  • Fold towels and stage toiletries neatly

Popular add-ons

  • Interior oven or fridge detail
  • Balcony or patio sweep and wipe-down
  • Baseboard detail or wall spot cleaning
  • Change bed linens in additional rooms
  • Laundry wash, dry, and fold (time-based)

Visit timing and preparation

Standard cleanings typically last 2 to 3 hours for most single-family homes. We send reminders before each visit and provide a care card with notes from your previous appointments.

  • Reminders 48 and 2 hours prior to arrival
  • Secure entry via code, lockbox, or key pickup
  • Same-day feedback and photo updates available
  • Discounted rates for a recurring schedule

Standard cleaning FAQs

How do you price a standard cleaning?
We consider square footage, number of bathrooms, floor type, and frequency. Share your layout for a fast, tailored quote.
Can I rotate rooms or tasks each visit?
Yes. We work from the core checklist but can alternate bonus rooms, guest suites, or special requests on a recurring rotation.
Do I need to provide supplies?
No. We arrive with vacuums, microfiber cloths, disinfectants, and eco-friendly options by request. Let us know if you prefer we use your products.
What if I need to reschedule?
Just give us 24 hours notice and we will move your visit. Recurring clients receive priority rescheduling spots.
