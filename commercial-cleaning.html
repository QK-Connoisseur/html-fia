Commercial cleaning checklist

Every scope is tailored, but these are the tasks most business clients include.

Common areas

  • Dust and wipe lobby furniture, fixtures, and signage
  • Vacuum carpets and mats, mop hard surface floors
  • Clean glass doors, partitions, and interior windows
  • Disinfect high-touch points: handles, railings, elevator buttons
  • Refresh waiting areas with tidy magazines and brochures

Workspaces

  • Sanitize desks, conference tables, and shared equipment
  • Empty trash and recycling, replace liners, and consolidate bins
  • Dust blinds, ledges, vents, and technology equipment
  • Spot clean walls and door frames in high-traffic corridors
  • Clean interior glass on offices and meeting rooms

Breakrooms and kitchens

  • Wipe counters, tables, appliance fronts, and cabinet doors
  • Sanitize sinks, faucets, and backsplash areas
  • Clean inside microwaves and empty drip trays
  • Restock paper towels, soap, and dish supplies
  • Maintain refrigerators with wipe-outs and expired item checks

Restrooms

  • Disinfect toilets, urinals, sinks, and fixtures
  • Polish mirrors, partitions, and stainless steel
  • Mop floors with disinfectant, focusing on grout lines
  • Restock tissue, soap, sanitizer, and feminine products
  • Document supply levels and maintenance concerns

Commercial cleaning FAQs

Do you carry insurance and bonding?
Yes. We are fully insured and bonded. Certificates of insurance can be issued to your property management team on request.
Can you support multiple locations?
Absolutely. We provide consistent teams across offices, retail suites, and clinics with centralized reporting.
What about supplies and consumables?
We can supply chemicals and equipment or use yours. We also track consumables like paper towels, soap, and liners so you never run out.
How do you measure quality?
Account managers perform scheduled inspections, share digital scorecards, and invite feedback after each visit.
