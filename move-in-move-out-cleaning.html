Move-in / Move-out checklist

Designed to satisfy landlord checklists and HOA requirements. We can tailor it to your property.

Whole property detail

  • Dust and wipe walls, trim, doors, and door frames
  • Clean interior windows, tracks, sliders, and sills
  • Wipe closets, shelving, and built-ins inside and out
  • Remove cobwebs, debris, and leftover nails or hooks
  • Polish light fixtures, vents, and ceiling fans

Kitchen turnover

  • Detail clean inside cabinets, drawers, and pantry shelving
  • Inside/outside of refrigerator, oven, microwave, and dishwasher
  • Degrease backsplash, stove hood, and appliance fronts
  • Scrub sink, garbage disposal, and countertops
  • Polish stainless steel and glass surfaces to streak-free shine

Bathrooms and laundry

  • Disinfect tubs, showers, tile, grout, and glass doors
  • Clean vanities, medicine cabinets, mirrors, and shelving
  • Sanitize toilets, including behind and around bases
  • Wipe washer, dryer, and utility sink inside and out
  • Remove lint and residue from dryer vent and filters

Final polish

  • Vacuum and mop every room, including closets and staircases
  • Wipe patio doors, balcony rails, and entry thresholds
  • Clean garage or storage areas (if empty)
  • Haul away small trash and recycling left behind
  • Leave a keyed hand-off report with timestamped photos

  • Flexible scheduling including evenings and weekends

Move-out cleaning FAQs

How much notice do you need?
Peak season fills quickly, so we recommend booking 1 to 2 weeks out. Ask about rush availability if you are on a tight timeline.
Do you clean furnished spaces?
Yes. Let us know what stays so we can adjust time for furniture, decor, or staging items.
Can you work while movers are present?
We coordinate around movers or carpet cleaners. For the fastest results, schedule us after large items have been removed.
Do you guarantee move-out inspection approval?
Our detailed checklist meets most landlord and property management standards. Share any specific requirements and we will confirm coverage.
