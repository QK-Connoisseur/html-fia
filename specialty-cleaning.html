Built for unique spaces and deadlines

Whether you manage a rental, completed a renovation, or need windows to sparkle inside and out, we scale crews to match the scope.

Specialty service checklists

Mix and match services to fit your project. We provide a written scope and on-site walkthrough before work begins.

Post-construction detail

  • Remove fine dust from walls, ceilings, vents, and fixtures
  • Vacuum and mop floors multiple times for a dust-free finish
  • Scrape paint, adhesive, and stickers from glass and surfaces
  • Polish cabinetry, countertops, and built-ins
  • Wipe electrical panels, outlets, and switch plates

Window and glass care

  • Interior and exterior glass (accessible up to two stories)
  • Clean tracks, sills, and screens
  • Remove hard water spots and light construction debris
  • Polish mirrors, railings, and glass partitions
  • Optional: high-reach poles for foyer or stairwell windows

Short-term rental turnovers

  • Hotel-style bedding, towel folds, and welcome staging
  • Inventory checks for linens, supplies, and toiletries
  • Photo documentation between guests
  • Restock coffee bars, snack stations, and guest amenities
  • Exterior touch-ups for patios, grills, and entryways

Custom projects

  • Garage, attic, or basement clean-outs
  • Stone, metal, or specialty surface polishing
  • Allergen reduction with HEPA vacuuming and air scrubbing
  • Event setup/tear-down cleaning support
  • Pressure washing coordination with trusted partners

Project planning and communication

Specialty jobs often involve multiple vendors. We provide a single point of contact, flexible team sizes, and real-time updates so you always know the status.

  • Pre-job safety briefing and scope confirmation
  • Check-in and check-out call or text updates
  • Before/after photos delivered digitally
  • Optional maintenance plan to protect your investment

Specialty cleaning FAQs

How far in advance should I book?
Specialty crews book quickly. Reserve 1 to 3 weeks ahead, especially for multi-day or construction projects.
Do you handle debris removal?
Light debris and packaging can be removed. For large items or dumpsters we coordinate with trusted hauling partners.
Can I combine services?
Yes. Mix windows, post-construction, rental turnovers, or event clean-ups into one customized visit.
Will you work with my contractor or property manager?
Absolutely. Share your timeline and point of contact so we can align schedules and punch lists.
